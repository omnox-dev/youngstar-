## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a reverent, community-focused, and celebratory digital experience for 'श्री गणेश मित्र मंडळ' (Ganesh Mitra Mandal). Rooted deeply in Maharashtrian cultural heritage, the visual identity balances sacred devotion with modern utility—offering clarity for aarti timings, prasad bookings, receipt management, and cultural event registrations.

### Design Direction & Style
- **Aesthetic Movement:** Classical Indic Contemporary (a synthesis of subtle tactile warmth, temple architectural motifs, and modern functionalism).
- **Cultural Texture:** Architectural cues inspired by Hemadpanthi temple arches, marigold garland (*zendu chi phule*) rhythms, and subtle ritual motifs like the modak, diya, and auspicious sacred trimmings.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Bhakti (devotion), Utsav (festive joy), and Vishwas (civic trust and transparency).
- **Execution Ethos:** Mobile-first, legible under bright sunlight for outdoor pandal visitors, hyper-accessible for elderly devotees reading Marathi Devanagari script, yet crisp and authoritative for administrative financial receipts and volunteer logs.

## Colors

The color palette captures the vibrant energy of Ganeshotsav while preserving clarity, dignity, and contrast.

### Palette Rationale
- **Primary — Saffron / Bhagwa (`#D9480F`, `#E05A10`, `#C2410C`):** The sacred color of dedication, morning mangal aarti, and celebration. Used for key call-to-actions, brand identity anchors, and prominent active navigation elements.
- **Secondary — Warm Festive Gold (`#D97706`, `#B45309`, `#F59E0B`):** Represents prosperity, brass ritual lamps (*samai*), and decorative borders. Used for badges, festive cards, and highlight indicators.
- **Tertiary — Pavitra Harit / Emerald Green (`#16A34A`):** The color of durva grass offered to Bappa; utilized strictly for success states, confirmed donation/paviti receipts, and verified volunteer badges.
- **Backgrounds — Mangal Cream (`#FFFDF9`, `#FBF8F2`, `#F8F4EA`):** Gentle, warm off-whites that mimic hand-made *chandan* parchment, eliminating stark digital glare for elderly community members.
- **Surfaces & Cards — Purity White (`#FFFFFF`):** High clarity surfaces enveloped by delicate saffron and gold hairline borders (`#FED7AA`, `#FDE68A`).
- **Text & Contrast — Deep Charcoal Maroon (`#2D1B18`):** Warmer and softer than pitch black, this ink-like tone guarantees AAA readability for complex Devanagari ligatures and conjunct characters (*jodakshare*).

## Typography

Typography prioritizes native Devanagari script integrity. Complex Marathi consonant conjuncts (*jodakshare*), upper matras (*kanha, matra, velanti*), and anuswaras require generous vertical breathing room.

- **Headline Hierarchy (`Noto Serif`):** Conveys the weight, solemnity, and gravitas of traditional festive invitations (*nimantran patrika*) and temple inscriptions.
- **Body & Controls (`Noto Sans`):** Provides extreme clarity across mobile screens, avoiding letterform crowding. Line heights are tuned ~15% taller than Latin baselines to prevent matra clipping.
- **Numerals & Receipts:** Financial amounts (₹), paviti tracking numbers, and queue counts are rendered with clear tabular lining figures for effortless legibility.

## Layout & Spacing

A cohesive 8pt spatial grid system organizes the interface with rhythmic pacing reminiscent of traditional Indian decorative friezes.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Mobile First Approach:** A 4-column layout on mobile devices (`320px` to `639px`) with `16px` gutters and `16px` lateral margins ensures thumb-friendly tap accessibility during crowded pandal conditions.
- **Tablet Reflow:** An 8-column layout (`640px` to `1023px`) accommodates side-by-side donation ledgers and live darshan feeds.
- **Desktop Architecture:** A 12-column grid capped at `1140px` centered within warm cream margins. Layout components conform cleanly to temple arch ratios, avoiding ultra-wide dispersed content.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids excessive plastic drop shadows, instead relying on layered paper levels, delicate golden linework, and soft ambient warmth.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Floor/Pitha):** Base canvas in warm off-white cream (`#FBF8F2`).
- **Level 1 (Patra / Surface Cards):** Pure white container backgrounds wrapped in crisp `1px` subtle golden-peach borders (`#FED7AA`). Ambient shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(45, 27, 24, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Mandap / Modals & Floating Shelves):** Lifted surfaces for Prasad Booking and Aarti Schedule drawers. Ambient shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(217, 72, 15, 0.12), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(45, 27, 24, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Alankaar / Popovers & Aarti Live Banners):** Pronounced elevation with subtle amber edge glows: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(180, 83, 9, 0.18), 0 0 0 1px #FDE68A`.

## Shapes

The geometric personality blends classic organic curves with structured framing. Base components feature an 8px (`0.5rem`) corner radius, maintaining a warm, welcoming presence without sliding into sterile roundedness or child-like pill forms.

Cards and hero modules selectively incorporate traditional Indian temple arch cusps (*kamani*) or stepped toran headers on top borders, grounding the modern cards in authentic cultural architecture.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Bhagwa Utsav):** Solid saffron fill (`#D9480F`), text in `#FFFFFF`, with a slight bottom rim border (`#B45309`). Active state triggers a subtle warm golden inner ring.
- **Secondary (Raja Suvarna):** Subtle warm cream fill with a 1.5px gold border (`#D97706`) and maroon text (`#2D1B18`).
- **Ghost / Text:** Deep saffron text accompanied by a miniature stylized diya or floral accent dot on hover.

### Receipts & Paviti Cards (पावती)
- Distinctive container styled like an official trust receipt: serrated or dashed dividing lines, watermark-style Om or Mandir silhouette, clear devotee details, and an authentic green verification badge (`#16A34A`).

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Compact components used for category filtering (उदा. आरती, देणगी, सांस्कृतिक कार्यक्रम, प्रसाद). Unselected: soft cream background with `#FED7AA` borders; Selected: rich saffron fill with high-contrast white Devanagari text.

### Inputs & Form Elements
- High-contrast inputs for mobile typing in both English and Devanagari IME keyboards. 
- Border is `1.5px` solid warm taupe (`#D1C7BD`), focusing into a prominent saffron highlight with a soft gold outer aura. Persistent labels placed above to support vertical diacritics.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Rounded-square checkboxes and circular radios utilizing saffron active fill with crisp white central check/dot glyphs. Form bounds maintain a minimum 44×44px interactive tap zone.

### Live Darshan & Aarti Card
- Media and schedule card highlighted by an ornamental marigold top-trim strip (`#F59E0B` dotted accent), dynamic time ticker in tabular figures, and an instant access action for streaming and push notifications.
